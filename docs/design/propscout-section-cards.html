Section cards — the agreed pattern (operator ruling 2026-10-28, option B). Yellow tags are annotations, not UI. Spec in words: .interface-design/system.md § Section cards. C1 card: --color-paper-2, 1px --color-rule, --radius-md, no shadow. C2 title = L2: Fraunces 600 17/24, ink, sentence case, never small caps. C3 title bar: padding 13 · 20 · 12 px, a --color-rule-soft line under it. C4 body: padding 16 · 20 · 18 px, 12 px between blocks. C5 16 px between cards. C6 on the title line: the count (mono 13, ink-3, a bare number, no brackets), then a note (12, ink-3) and the actions, pushed right. C7 a group inside a card = L3 (Inter 600 14) over a --color-rule-soft line, never a box. C8 small caps only above a value, a control or a table column (L4). C9 no box around a group or an empty state inside a card (Valuo’s dashed box goes); a figure tile (EstimateCard) stays. C10 an empty section keeps its card and count 0, with one plain line and the action. X1 the extension panel uses the same card in its own tokens: --paper on --bg, 1px --rule, 3px radius, title bar 9 · 12 · 8, title 600 14/20 in the panel’s font, body 10 · 12 · 12, 10 px between cards. Drawn in the PropScout.cz design system’s components and Měď light; build it with the app’s own tokens and components.
